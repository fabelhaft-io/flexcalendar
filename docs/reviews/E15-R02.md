### The rules the expected values come from (cite them by section; check the RFC text yourself, do not trust this paragraph)

- RFC 5545 § 3.3.5, form 3: a local time that occurs more than once (fall back) refers to the **first** occurrence; a local time that does not occur (spring forward) is interpreted with the UTC offset **before** the gap. Its own examples are `TZID=America/New_York:20071104T013000` (EDT) and `20070311T023000` (3:30 EDT).
- RFC 5545 § 3.3.10: a recurrence rule may generate an instance at a nonexistent local time; such an instance **MUST be ignored and MUST NOT be counted** in the recurrence set. (So a daily 02:30 Berlin series has no instance on 2026-03-29, and `COUNT` does not spend one on it. This differs from the single-`DTSTART` rule above: say so in the table.)
- RFC 5545 § 3.3.4 and § 3.8.2.2: an all-day event is a `DATE`, `DTEND` exclusive; it is not midnight-to-midnight. Its instants exist only in a viewing zone, as that zone's local midnights: 23, 24 or 25 hours apart.
- A recurrence keeps its **local** time across a transition, so the UTC instants of a 09:00 series shift by an hour (§ 3.8.5.3; the instance is generated in the local time of `DTSTART`).
