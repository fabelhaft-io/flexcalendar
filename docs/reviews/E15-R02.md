### Independent checks

- **An oracle of my own** (Python `zoneinfo`, PEP 495 `fold`, no ICU, nothing of the implementer's): it derives every expected instant from the inputs under § 3.3.5 and the "ignored and not counted" reading of § 3.3.10: **104 of 104 equal the table**. It scans the tz database for the real 2026 and 2027 transitions of the six zones (Berlin 29 Mar/25 Oct and 28 Mar/31 Oct, New York 8 Mar/1 Nov and 14 Mar/7 Nov, Sydney 5 Apr/4 Oct and 4 Apr/3 Oct, Chatham 5 Apr/27 Sep and 4 Apr/26 Sep, none for São Paulo and Kolkata) and finds an all-day case on every one. Not vacuous: a repeated time given its second occurrence, a counted skipped instance and a deleted transition day each fail it.
- **The gate bites.** 11 mutations of `cases.json` (second occurrence for a repeated time; a counted skipped instance; the offset after the gap; an all-day end an hour late; a view-zone instant an hour off; a São Paulo control shifted on the old rule's date; Chatham removed; every 2027 Sydney case removed; one `rfc` stripped; the New York 2027 repeated case removed; a series one instance short): all fail `dst-table.mjs`, each naming the case or the missing coverage.
- **The README's engine table reproduces.** I ran `ical.js` 2.2.1 and `sabre/vobject` 4.6.1 myself over the Berlin and New York cases: every cell matches (`ical.js` gives 00:30Z for the skipped single time, both give 01:30Z for the repeated one, and both count the skipped recurrence instance, at 00:30Z and 01:30Z).
- **The RFC conflict is real.** I read § 3.3.10 in full: "MUST be ignored and MUST NOT be counted" in the first paragraph, and the last paragraph says the computed local start time of an instance that does not exist, or occurs more than once, "is interpreted in the same manner as an explicit DATE-TIME value" (§ 3.3.5). My brief quoted only the first. Eight `daily-skipped` cases differ between the readings (my oracle prints the second reading for each).

### Fix requests (one run)

1. **Mark the contested cases in `cases.json`, not only in the README.** A runner reads the table, not the prose. Give each `daily-skipped` case `"ruling": "pending"` and `"alternative"`: the instants under the other reading (the instance kept, at the offset before the gap, counted). The validator requires both on that kind, derives `alternative` from ICU like `expected`, and fails when they are equal. `rfc` cites both sentences of § 3.3.10. The README says what a runner does with `ruling: pending`: accepts `expected` or `alternative`, reports which, and accepts nothing else. When the project rules, one commit removes the field and one list. Test that the validator fails without the field and on a wrong `alternative`.
2. **`view-zone` must pin what the viewer shows.** The 12 cases carry only the UTC instant, which no wrong viewer can fail. Add `expectedLocal`, the wall-clock reading in `viewZone` as `YYYYMMDDTHHMMSS` (Berlin 2026-03-29 09:00 viewed from New York is `20260329T030000`), derive it from ICU in the validator, and add the mutation test (an offset off by an hour in the view zone fails).

Change nothing else.
