### An independent oracle for the resolver and the generated `VTIMEZONE`

Python `zoneinfo` (PEP 495) and a `VTIMEZONE` parser of my own, nothing shared with the module: 56 zones (the six of the table, Kathmandu +05:45, Lord Howe's 30-minute DST, Apia's skipped day, Kiritimati, St John's, Casablanca, Dublin, Troll, Caracas, Tehran, Santiago, Moscow, Pyongyang, UTC, `Etc/GMT+5` and 24 random others), **178,968 `instantOf` samples** (every transition of every zone from 1970 to 2100 in 15-minute steps across ±3½ hours, plus 300 random readings per zone) and **44,760 `localOf` samples**, 56 generated definitions and 634 zone names.
- Zero mismatches in 54 zones. The two that differ are **Africa/Casablanca** (from 2026-09) and **Asia/Tehran** (1979): Node's ICU carries tz 2026b and this host's `/usr/share/zoneinfo` 2026e, which changed those two zones. That is data drift, not logic, and a follow-up (zone data versions across components).
- Every generated definition has only allowed properties (`TZID`, `X-LIC-LOCATION`; `DTSTART`, `RDATE`, `TZOFFSETFROM`, `TZOFFSETTO`, `TZNAME`), no `RRULE`, and my parser reads the right offset at every transition ±1 minute and at 400 random instants per zone (Europe/Berlin 5.9 KB, New York 6.4 KB, UTC 178 bytes, the largest 7.3 KB).
- The 634 names: accepted exactly where `zoneinfo` lists them (`Asia/Kolkata`, `Asia/Calcutta`, `US/Eastern` included); `europe/berlin`, `EUROPE/BERLIN`, trailing or leading spaces, a newline or NUL, `Europe/../etc/passwd`, `Mars/Olympus`, the empty string and eight Windows names (`W. Europe Standard Time`, `Eastern Standard Time`, …) refused.

### The live stack (fresh start, through Caddy with Caddy's CA, no `-k`)

- Writes with a zone → `201`, `parsed` reads `{local, tzid, utc}`: Berlin 10:00 → 08:00Z; **a repeated reading** Berlin 2026-10-25 02:30 → `00:30Z` and New York 2026-11-01 01:30 → `05:30Z` (the first occurrence, the RFC's answer where `ical.js` says the second); **a nonexistent reading as the start** Berlin 2026-03-29 02:30 → `01:30Z` and New York 2026-03-08 02:30 → `07:30Z` (the offset before the gap; `ical.js` gives `00:30Z` and `06:30Z`). Two zones in one object embed two definitions. Link names and odd offsets (Calcutta, `US/Eastern`, Kathmandu +05:45, Lord Howe, Chatham) give the right instants.
- Refused with the fixed `422` problem: eight Windows or unknown or mis-cased names (and the empty one, a space, a trailing space, `Europe/../x`, `GMT+1`, `Z`), a floating `local`, `tzid` beside `utc`, an end before the start across zones (Berlin 10:00 to New York 03:00), an hour of `25:00`, `2026-02-30`, and a reading before 1970 or after 2100 (the horizon the embedded definition covers; a `utc` time is written whatever the date). `UNTIL` as a date beside a zone start → `422`, as a UTC instant → `201`.
- Patches: the end moved to Kolkata embeds a second definition and `parsed.end` reads 12:30Z; a start that lands after the end → `422`; the start back to `utc` → `200`.
