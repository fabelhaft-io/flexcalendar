2. **`UNTIL` and the floating copy:** the live repro now answers 3 of 3 for the New York series with `UNTIL` at its third instance. The oracle (Python `dateutil.rrule` plus `zoneinfo`, nothing shared) ran **11,500 objects with and without an embedded `VTIMEZONE`** (zoned, UTC, floating and all-day masters; `UNTIL` at, one second either side of and away from an instance; `Z` `RDATE`/`EXDATE`; overrides; windows on instance edges; 13 zones, 9 viewing zones, starts on transition days): the only 2 mismatches are my generator's, an event whose `DTEND` falls before its `DTSTART` across a gap (the module reads a zero-length event, which is right).
