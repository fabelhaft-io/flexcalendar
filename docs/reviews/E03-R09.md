## Brief (condensed)

Guard the **backends**, not HTTP events: `Backend\PDO::createCalendar/updateCalendar/createCalendarObject/updateCalendarObject` and the PropertyStorage `propPatch`, because `COPY`, cross-collection `MOVE`, the extended `MKCOL`, jCal and `REPAIR` bypass or rewrite what PUT-time hooks see (read in sabre/dav 4.7.1 at `f36f002`, not run; the probes and the `COPY`/`MOVE` mutation confirm it). Mode per call from `public.calendars`; no row or a non-UUID segment means Private; a database error fails closed. A pure allow-list over `sabre/vobject`, exactly E03-R09's table. The exception is a `Forbidden` subclass serialized by `Service::errorResponse` (preconditions only), with the name echoed only inside `[A-Z][A-Z0-9-]{0,63}`. Tests: a table-driven unit suite; `GuardTest` over the delegation header; markers `CALFLEX_GUARD_MARKER_<CASE>` in every rejected value, scanned in responses by PHPUnit and in the CalDAV log by `make test-caldav` (its regexp extended). The fixture became a valid skeleton. No new dependency, service or variable.
