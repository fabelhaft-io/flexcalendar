### The six fixes of review 1

1. **INV-05.** The spec no longer names the two properties; `spec-recurrence-scan.test.mjs` applies the invariant's own expression to it. Putting the word back into one description fails it (checked), and `make verify-tooling` passes again on the regenerated clients.
2. **Only valid objects.** `assertOrder` runs on the merged object after a create and after a patch; value types must match and the end must be later. A pair it cannot compare without zone arithmetic (a `Z` instant beside a `TZID` wall clock, or two zones) is deliberately left to E05-R09, which keeps every corpus fixture patchable.
3. **Only valid, safe rules.** Exactly the RFC's rule parts, once each, `FREQ` first; unknown parts, `COUNT` with `UNTIL`, `UNTIL` of the wrong type, `BYWEEKNO` and a yearly 29 February are refused. Beyond what I asked, `COUNT=0`, `INTERVAL=0`, a bad `WKST` and an impossible `UNTIL` date are refused too, because `ical.js` would drop or roll them; a test pins this against the whole `rrule/` corpus (32 rules accepted, exactly the two ruled fixtures refused).
4. **Empty `PATCH`** is a `422` and writes nothing.
5. **A vanished object.** The double answers `412` to `If-Match` on a missing object as SabreDAV does (confirmed live: it is the real behaviour), and the re-read turns it into the `404`.
6. **Framework errors** are problems: `frameworkErrors` answers an over-long parameter as the fixed `not-found` and a malformed URL as `bad-request`; E11 is dead.
